Welcome to Alaska Cannabis Exchange

Your first visit, made easy.

New to the shop or just want to feel prepared? Here are a few simple ways to make your visit comfortable and help you find the information you need.

Explore our products

A few helpful steps

Take your time. Ask questions.

There’s no need to know all the terminology before you arrive. Start with what matters to you, and our team can help you explore the options.

01

Think about what you’re looking for

Consider the product type or experience you’re curious about. If you’re unsure, that’s a good place to start a conversation.

02

Ask the team

Our staff can walk you through product information, formats, and the questions to consider when comparing options.

03

Review details at your pace

Take a moment to read labels and product information, and choose only when you feel comfortable with your decision.

A quick note before you visit

Please review the store’s current entry requirements and local regulations before you arrive. Product details are available from the team in store.
